# Template conventions (from Jorge, 2026-07-11)

Category labels in the menu costing template and what they mean:

- **Pre Ingredient** — something cooked/prepared on site (e.g., shredded beef,
  cooked black beans, guasacaca). Has its own recipe of raw inputs. Cost per
  unit = batch input cost / batch yield. These feed into Ready Dishes.
- **Raw and Ready** — purchased and used as-is (frozen tequeños, cheese wheels,
  candy, canned drinks). Cost comes directly from the vendor invoice.
- **Ready Dish** — the final dish exactly as served to the customer, assembled
  from Pre Ingredients and Raw and Ready items. This is what carries the menu
  price and margin.
- **Water** (and similar free items) — never counted, zero cost.
